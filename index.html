PID Control of a Thruster-Actuated Inverted Pendulum

Modeled, linearized, and controlled a pneumatic thruster-driven arrow ("Wheel of Despair") — derived the nonlinear equations of motion, fit thruster and damping dynamics to test data, designed a Root-Locus-based PID controller in MATLAB, and validated it in Simulink and on the physical rig.
