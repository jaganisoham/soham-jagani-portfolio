Selected Work

PID Control of a Thruster-Actuated Inverted Pendulum

Modeled, linearized, and controlled a pneumatic thruster-driven arrow ("Wheel of Despair") — derived the nonlinear equations of motion, fit thruster and damping dynamics to test data, designed a Root-Locus-based PID controller in MATLAB, and validated it in Simulink and on the physical rig.

SOFTWARE
MATLAB, Simulink
DOMAIN
Controls
METHOD
PID, Root Locus
TYPE
Design + Hardware Test
RESULT
0 s.s. error @ ±100°, 180°
SETTLING
4.5s (5% band)

Kirigami Gripper for Sweet Potato Stem Planting

Undergraduate research in the Yin Lab — designed a cut-pattern soft gripper to grip and plant stem-like cuttings, iterating on pinch force and flexibility through cut-pattern and material changes.

SOFTWARE
SolidWorks, Laser Cutting
DOMAIN
Soft Robotics
ADVISOR
Dr. Jie Yin
TYPE
Research + Prototype
RESULT
Slip-free grip on cylindrical loads
STATUS
